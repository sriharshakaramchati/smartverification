BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is available today?

The button opens Reclaim's existing verification flow. Available checks are determined there. DigiLocker, identity, address, reference and court-record integrations have not been activated on this website.

Is every background check instant?

No. Supported source proofs can be automated; source availability, identity matching and manual checks affect completion. No turnaround guarantee is offered here.

Can we recheck existing employees?

Yes, the service can be scoped for employee rechecks. Define the purpose and frequency and obtain the appropriate authorisation for the checks requested.

Does a cryptographic proof guarantee a clean background?

No. It establishes information returned by the configured source. Findings must be interpreted within the check's coverage and limitations.
